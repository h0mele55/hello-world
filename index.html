Moving Business Forward

Headquartered in Lovech, Bulgaria, SP Transport has established itself as a premier logistics provider. We specialize in international road transport, groupage shipments, and comprehensive supply chain solutions across the European Union, Scandinavia, and the Balkans.

With over a decade of industry expertise, an EU-licensed modern fleet, and a dedicated team of dispatchers, we ensure that your cargo arrives safely, on time, and on budget.

  • ISO 9001 Certified Quality Standards
  • 24/7 Satellite Tracking & Dispatch
  • Multi-lingual Support Team

Our Services

We provide tailored transportation and warehousing solutions to meet the specific needs of your industry.

Road Transport (FTL)

Full Truck Load services across Europe. We operate a modern fleet of Euro 6 standard trucks, ensuring rapid and eco-friendly delivery.

Groupage & LTL

Cost-effective groupage lines connecting Bulgaria with Italy, Germany, the UK, and Scandinavia. Regular weekly departures.

ADR / Dangerous Goods

Fully licensed and equipped vehicles for the safe transportation of hazardous materials, handled by specially trained and certified drivers.

Warehousing & Logistics

Secure, strategically located warehousing facilities. Offering cross-docking, palletization, and comprehensive inventory management.

Multimodal Forwarding

Integration of road, sea, and rail transport to optimize your supply chain costs, providing flexible and scalable freight solutions.

Customs Clearance

Comprehensive customs agency services, INTRASTAT processing, and documentation assistance for seamless import/export operations.
